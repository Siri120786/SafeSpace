## Limitations to state at sprint review
- Module screens and the story workflow **keep data only while the page is open**. Nothing is saved to the database yet, because that needs new tables and policies owned by Siri.
- Story approval is real in the screen's logic (only a caregiver can approve) but is not enforced by the database yet.
- PCOS / PCOD is now a hub with three spaces: **Cycle Care** (check-in, medicine notes and reminders, doctor-visit prep, sleep and stress), **Physical Exercise** (daily minutes, yoga time, weekly workout plan, optional weekly goal) and **Food** (diet plan, rewards, character, water). The person picks PCOS, PCOD or Not sure, and the wording changes; every feature exists for all three. **No cycle or period tracking**, pending privacy review (the health profile only asks how periods have been lately). The diet plan, rewards, movement log, weekly plan, health profile and chosen label are kept **on the device only** (browser storage on web, shared_preferences on Flutter), never in the account; check-ins, notes and the water tally are not saved. Rewards (stars and streaks) were chosen by the team and can be hidden. Safeguards that stay: the person writes the diet plan, the app never counts calories, sets a target weight, works out BMI or interprets blood values, the character never shames or mentions the body, rest days are part of the plan and the exercise goal is optional. Tests check these. Medicine reminders use the shared Reminders feature with a generic title ("Time for your medicine"): in-app alerts only, web first. Flutter shows a note instead until a reminders service exists on mobile. Flutter's character is a drawn icon; the web one is the BrainMascot, and only web has a "Hear it" button (speech). The old route `/modules/cycle-care` redirects to `/modules/pcos-pcod/cycle-care`.
- No reminders are scheduled and no notifications are sent.
- Flutter versions of the story workflow and all four modules exist (`apps/mobile/lib/features/story_workflow/`, `wellness_modules/`) with the same not-saved limitation. They pass `flutter analyze` (no new issues) and `flutter test`, but have not been run on a real device or emulator.
